selected publications

  1. Drivevlm: The convergence of autonomous driving and large vision-language models
    2024
    Abs HTML

    This work presents DriveVLM, a novel framework that integrates large vision-language models for autonomous driving. We demonstrate how VLMs can enhance perception, prediction, and planning in complex driving scenarios.

  2. Street gaussians: Modeling dynamic urban scenes with gaussian splatting
    2024
    Abs HTML

    We propose Street Gaussians, a new explicit scene representation for modeling dynamic urban street scenes. Our method achieves high-quality rendering of complex urban environments with dynamic objects.

  3. Planagent: A multi-modal large language agent for closed-loop vehicle motion planning
    2024
    Abs HTML

    PlanAgent leverages multi-modal large language models for vehicle motion planning. Our approach achieves superior performance in complex driving scenarios through language-guided planning.

  4. Tod3cap: Towards 3d dense captioning in outdoor scenes
    2024
    Abs HTML

    Tod3Cap introduces a novel framework for 3D dense captioning in outdoor scenes, enabling detailed scene understanding through natural language descriptions of 3D environments.
